How a dose is scored
  • FITS judges each dose against one vaccine group (its relatedTo vaccine). CDSi evaluates each dose per antigen. The engine's verdict for the dose is rolled up over the antigens in that group that the administered vaccine carries, with precedence not valid > sub-standard > valid > extraneous: any antigen calling it not valid makes it not valid; a dose that counted for at least one antigen is valid. The Logic Specification defines no group-level dose verdict, so this rollup is a test-harness choice - doses where the antigens disagree are tagged below.
  • Not evaluated: the engine never evaluated the dose for any antigen in the group (for example, the vaccine isn't associated with the antigen at the patient's age). It counts as a miss, not as invalid.
  • Extraneous is kept separate and not counted as invalid in the rates above.
  • FITS deliberately over-samples invalid doses, so the rates transfer to production data better than raw counts or the share of invalid doses do.
