2026-09-08 - Phase 21 - Role B Round 9 - Note 2a's Age-Based CVX-to-Antigen Association reviewed

The ninth Role B round: section 4.2's note 2a (age-based CVX-to-antigen association - e.g. CVX 121 Zoster live associates with Varicella under 50 years, Zoster from 50 on) was entirely unimplemented, a complete gap rather than a logic bug. 2026-09-08 - Phase 21 - Role B Round 11 - Chapter 8's Series-Group Loop (Merged Despite a FITS Regression) reviewed

The eleventh Role B round, and the largest of this campaign: Chapter 8 (8.1-8.8) never implemented the per-series-group repetition its own overview text requires, and the eight steps disagreed about which of three different lists was "the series in scope" - the 2026-09-05 "Chapter 8 has no series group" cross-cutting entry. Implemented a new SelectNextSeriesGroup step (dispatched from 4.5, looped back to by every 8.1-8.7 scoring-phase exit), corrected the pipeline-stage list every affected step reads, and parsed the previously-unread <equivalentSeriesGroups> Supporting Data element 8.8's Table 8-14 needs. Two independent unit-local bugs (8.5/8.6's sticky-boolean defect, 8.6's tie-counter reset) were fixed alongside. Verified against all nine affected units' own JUnit suites - every remaining red in each reconciles exactly against an already-documented, unrelated, pre-existing defect (confirmed by cross-referencing this round's failures against each unit's own status.yaml red-test enumeration). Full cdsi-engine suite: 167 -> 139 failures (-28), 0 new errors. FITS regresses: 3378 -> 3253 passed (net -125: 175 cases regress, mostly Pneumococcal/RSV/Meningococcal; 50 improve, mostly HepA/Polio/MMR). Traced via direct engine instrumentation to a second, separate, upstream defect - SelectPatientSeries.minAgeToStart/maxAgeToStart are enforced nowhere in the engine, so an age-inappropriate solo-series series group (e.g. "Pneumococcal 50+", minAgeToStart 50 years) can now become a "best patient series" once Chapter 8 is correctly scoped, where before it simply lost an undivided antigen-wide scoring competition. Recorded as SPEC-4.6-0021, not yet fixed - its most likely home is upstream of Chapter 8 entirely (4.3/5.1's determination of what counts as a relevant patient series in the first place). Presented to the project owner as a genuine choice (hold pending SPEC-4.6-0021, keep investigating a same-session fix, or merge despite the regression); the explicit decision was to merge now, accepting the regression, because the fix is independently verified correct, the regression's cause is fully traced to a separate pre-existing defect rather than an error in this fix, and the 50-case improvement it also produces is real forward progress expected to combine cleanly with SPEC-4.6-0021's eventual fix. Recorded as a deliberate, informed exception to the standing "FITS moves forward, not backward" rule, for CDC/CDSi review.
