SELECTB-6  
A scorable patient series must be considered a complete patient series if the patient series forecast made 
from the scorable patient series has a patient series status of 'Complete'.  
SELECTB-19  
A scorable patient series has the most valid doses if the number of valid doses is the same as or greater 
than the number of valid doses in all other scorable patient series.
