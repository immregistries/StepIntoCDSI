# Chapter 4.5: Select Best Patient Series

## Summary
Iterates through selected antigens and identifies candidate patient series for each antigen. Filters series by target disease and sets up for pre-filtering and prioritization scoring process.

## Methods
- `process(): LogicStep` — Increments antigen position; if more antigens remain, filters and processes next antigen; otherwise transitions to IDENTIFY_AND_EVALUATE_VACCINE_GROUP
- `printTableAndFigures(PrintWriter): void` — Displays SELECT BEST PATIENT SERIES process steps and workflow diagram

## ConditionAttributes Evaluated
- Antigen (current)
- AntigenSeries (filtered by target disease)
- PatientSeries (filtered by target disease)
- BestPatientSeriesList

## Key Data Structures
- `Antigen` — Current disease antigen
- `AntigenSeries` — Series definitions for antigen
- `AntigenSeriesSelectedList` — Filtered series for antigen
- `PatientSeries` — Patient-specific series instances
- `SelectedPatientSeriesList` — Series candidates for selection

## Process Steps (from printTableAndFigures)
1. Identify Superior Patient Series
2. Classify Patient Series
3. Score Patient Series (Complete, In-Process, No Valid Doses)
4. Select Best Patient Series
