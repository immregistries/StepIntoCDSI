# Chapter 7.4: Determine Forecast Need

## Summary
Evaluates whether patient requires another dose of the vaccine, considering administered history, evidence of immunity, contraindications, and other factors.

## Methods
- `process(): LogicStep` — Evaluates forecast need logic; sets forecast status; transitions to GENERATE_FORECAST_DATES_AND_RECOMMENDED_VACCINES
- `printPre(PrintWriter): void` — Pre-processing output
- `printPost(PrintWriter): void` — Post-processing output

## ConditionAttributes Evaluated
- Vaccine dose(s) administered (caVaccineDoseAdministered)
- Target dose statuses (caTargetDoseStatuses)
- Seasonal recommendation end date (caSeasonalRecommendationEndDate)
- Evidence of immunity (from 7.2)
- Contraindicated patient series (from 7.3)
- Assessment date (caAssessmentDate)
- Maximum age date (caMaximumAgeDate, CALCDTAGE-1)
- Candidate earliest date (FORECASTDTCAN-1)

## Forecast Need Determination
- Patient age at assessment
- Number of doses already satisfied
- Series requirements (minimum doses needed)
- Maximum age for series
- Seasonal recommendation windows
- Evidence of immunity status
- Contraindication status

## Decision Factors
1. Has patient received required minimum doses? → May be complete
2. Is patient past maximum age? → Series closed
3. Is patient within seasonal window? → Timing consideration
4. Any contraindications? → Cannot forecast
5. Evidence of immunity? → Forecast not needed
