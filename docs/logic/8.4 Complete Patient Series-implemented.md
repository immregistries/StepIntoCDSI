# Chapter 8.4: Complete Patient Series

## Summary
Scores complete patient series by applying scoring rules for series that have satisfied all required doses. Primary scoring based on count of valid doses.

## Methods
- `process(): LogicStep` — Evaluates completion scoring logic; applies scores to series
- `numberOfValidDoses(PatientSeries)`: Counts SATISFIED target doses
- `evaluate_ACandidatePatientSeriesHasTheMostValidDoses()`: Scores by valid dose count

## Scoring Phase 1: Find Maximum Valid Doses
- Iterate through COMPLETE series
- Count satisfied doses per series
- Track maximum dose count found

## Scoring Phase 2: Score by Valid Doses
- Series with maximum valid doses: Score +2
- Series with fewer valid doses: Score -2
- Compares against "mostValidDoses"

## Complete Series Criteria
- PatientSeriesStatus = COMPLETE
- All required doses satisfied
- Ready for final best series determination

## Key Data Structures
- `PatientSeries` — Series with COMPLETE status
- `TargetDose` — Satisfied vs unsatisfied
- Scoring: `incPatientScoreSeries()`, `descPatientScoreSeries()`

## Scoring Output
- Each series has score value
- Higher score = more likely to be selected
- Used in subsequent prioritization
