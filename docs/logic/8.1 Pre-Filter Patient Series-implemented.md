# Chapter 8.1: Pre-Filter Patient Series

## Summary
Filters candidate patient series to only those with at least one satisfied dose. Separates non-contraindicated from contraindicated schedules, and identifies highest risk priority for risk-based series.

## Methods
- `process(): LogicStep` — Filters patient series by status and dose validity; categorizes into scorable list; transitions to IDENTIFY_ONE_PRIORITIZED_PATIENT_SERIES
- Filters contraindicated vs. non-contraindicated schedules
- Identifies highest risk priority series

## ConditionAttributes Evaluated
- Patient series status (CONTRAINDICATED, COMPLETE, IN_PROCESS, NO_VALID_DOSES)
- Target dose status (SATISFIED)
- Evaluation status (VALID)
- Series type (RISK, ROUTINE)
- Series priority

## Filtering Logic
1. If any non-contraindicated series available: Use those
2. If no non-contraindicated: Fall back to contraindicated series
3. Among risk series: Track highest priority
4. Filter to series with at least one valid dose

## Key Data Structures
- `PatientSeries` — Candidate series with status
- `TargetDose` — Dose satisfaction tracking
- `Evaluation` — Dose validity tracking
- `SeriesType` — RISK vs ROUTINE classification
- `ScorablePatientSeriesList` — Output of pre-filtering

## Output
- Sets dataModel.setScorablePatientSeriesList() with filtered results
- Prepares for prioritization and scoring phases
