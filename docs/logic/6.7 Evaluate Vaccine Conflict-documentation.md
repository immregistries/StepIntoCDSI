## Chapter Text

Evaluate vaccine conflict validates the date administered of a vaccine dose administered against previous administered vaccines to ensure proper spacing between administrations. This covers live virus vaccine conflict s as well as non - live virus vaccine conflicts . Many vaccines do not have any conflict with each other . Therefore, if no vaccine Supporting Data exists for the vaccine type of the vaccine dose administered being evaluated, the vaccine dose administered is not in conflict with any other vaccine dose administered.
