Select prioritized patient series provides the business rules to be applied to the scored patient series which will result in the prioritized patient series for the series group.

## Table 8 - 13 Select Prioritized Patient Series Business Rules

| Business Rule ID | Business Rule |
|---|---|
| SELECTBEST-1 | The scorable patient series score must be the sum of all points awarded to the scorable patient series. |
| SELECTBEST-2 | The prioritized patient series must be one of the following: The scorable patient series with the highest scorable patient series score. The scorable patient series with the best ranked series preference if more than one scorable patient series are tied for the highest scorable patient series score. |
