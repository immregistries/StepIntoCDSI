# Chapter 9.2: Single Antigen Vaccine Group

## Summary
Processes vaccine groups containing exactly one antigen. Directly applies antigen-level forecast results to vaccine group forecast with minimal transformation.

## Methods
- `process(): LogicStep` — Iterates through best patient series, matches antigen forecasts, creates vaccine group forecast, transitions to IDENTIFY_AND_EVALUATE_VACCINE_GROUP
- Evaluates antigen match for vaccine group
- Applies SINGLEANTVG business rules

## Processing Logic
1. Get primary antigen from vaccine group (only antigen)
2. Iterate through best patient series list
3. Find series with matching antigen in forecast
4. Apply SINGLEANTVG-1 rule: Vaccine group status = patient series status

## Key Business Rules
- **SINGLEANTVG-1**: Vaccine group status matches patient series status
  - COMPLETE → Vaccine group COMPLETE
  - IN_PROCESS → Vaccine group IN_PROCESS
  - NO_VALID_DOSES → Vaccine group NO_VALID_DOSES

## Output Creation
- `VaccineGroupForecast` created for vaccine group
- Forecast details copied from best patient series
- Status and dates transferred directly

## Key Data Structures
- `VaccineGroup` — Single-antigen group
- `Antigen` — Only antigen in group
- `PatientSeries` — Best series list
- `Forecast` — Antigen-level forecast
- `VaccineGroupForecast` — Output

## Error Handling
- Alerts if vaccine group or antigen missing
- Skips forecast if antigen not found in best series
