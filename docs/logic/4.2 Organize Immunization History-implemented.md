# Chapter 4.2: Organize Immunization History

## Summary
Breaks down administered vaccines into their antigen components and creates AntigenAdministeredRecords. Records are sorted first by antigen name, then by administration date to prepare data for granular evaluation and forecasting.

## Methods
- `process(): LogicStep` — Iterates through vaccine doses, extracts antigens, creates AntigenAdministeredRecords, sorts by antigen then date, then transitions to CREATE_RELEVANT_PATIENT_SERIES
- `printPre(PrintWriter): void` — Explains the purpose of organizing by antigen
- `printPost(PrintWriter): void` — Displays before/after tables of vaccine organization

## ConditionAttributes Evaluated
- Vaccine doses administered (from ImmunizationHistory)
- Vaccine types and associated antigens
- Administration dates for sorting

## Key Data Structures
- `AntigenAdministeredRecord` — Maps administered vaccines to individual antigens with dates
- `Antigen` — Individual disease antigens from vaccines
- `VaccineDoseAdministered` — Original vaccine dose records
- `VaccineType` — Contains list of antigens per vaccine

## Sorting Logic
Primary: Antigen name (ascending alphabetical)
Secondary: Administration date (ascending chronological)
