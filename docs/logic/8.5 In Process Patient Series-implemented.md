# Chapter 8.5: In Process Patient Series

## Summary
Scores in-process patient series (partial doses satisfied) using multiple criteria including product path alignment and completability.

## Methods
- `process(): LogicStep` — Evaluates in-process scoring conditions [partial implementation]
- `evaluate_ACandidatePatientSeriesIsAProductPatientSeriesAndHasAllValidDoses()`: Scores product series with valid doses
- [Additional conditions under development]

## Scoring Conditions
1. Is series a product path series AND has all valid doses?
   - YES: +2, -2 score modification
   - NO: Penalty

2. Is series completable?
   - Series can reach completion status

3. Earlier forecast date?
   - Series can start sooner

4. Other scoring factors

## Product Path Series
- Series defined for specific product preference
- May have priority over generic series
- Score +2 if has all valid doses
- Score -2 if not product path or missing doses

## Scoring Output
- In-process series scored relative to complete series
- Enables prioritization if no complete series available
- Used for "catch-up" scenarios

## Key Data Structures
- `PatientSeries` — Series with IN_PROCESS status
- `TargetDose` — Partial satisfaction
- `AntigenSeries.SelectPatientSeries` — Product path flag
- `TimePeriod` — Age and interval tracking
